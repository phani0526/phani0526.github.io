I started my career in production support at CGI, on a team responsible for
fifteen Java/WebLogic applications behind Bell Canada's call-centre and TV
ecosystem. That meant 24×7 incident bridges, weekly deployments and a lot of
repetitive manual checking. The repetition is what pulled me into automation:
I joined a four-person core team building CGI's Intelligent Automation
Program, and over the next two years shipped more than 110 automations across
30 applications.

At Juniper Networks I moved from running systems to building them. I design
and own internal release-engineering platforms in Python and Django that
consolidate build histories, pull-request mappings and branch metadata for
teams around the world. Most of my work sits at the joins between systems:
Git and SVN, Jenkins, MySQL and NFS, and making the data flowing between them
correct and traceable.

I care about the unglamorous parts of engineering: data integrity,
access control, audit readiness and documentation. I've found they're what
decide whether a tool is trusted or quietly worked around.

Outside the day job I enjoy explaining how this work actually gets done. I've
run workshops and onboarding sessions for more than ten teams, and I'm keen to
do more of that with students, meetups and engineering organisations.
