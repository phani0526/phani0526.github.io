### Short (about 50 words)

Phaneendra Reddy Dwaracharla is a Software Engineer 3 at Juniper Networks in
Bengaluru, where he builds Python/Django release-engineering platforms used by
teams worldwide. Over nine years he has moved from production support to
platform engineering, with a focus on operations automation, data integrity
and dependable internal tools.

### Long (about 150 words)

Phaneendra Reddy Dwaracharla is a backend and release engineer with nine years
of experience in production systems. At Juniper Networks he designs and runs
internal release-engineering platforms in Python and Django that consolidate
build histories, pull-request data and branch metadata, automate thousands of
release requests a year, and feed a build-failure analysis system that helped
cut recurring failures by more than half.

Before Juniper, he spent four years at CGI supporting mission-critical telecom
applications for Bell Canada and was part of the core team behind CGI's
Intelligent Automation Program, delivering more than 110 automations across
30 applications. His work there earned the CGI APEX Gold Award.

Phaneendra holds a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a
PG Diploma in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. He enjoys teaching
practical engineering and has led workshops for more than ten teams.
