### From runbook to automation, without losing control

How to decide which operational tasks to automate, build automations that
fail safely, and roll them out across many applications. Based on CGI's
Intelligent Automation Program, where I helped ship 110+ automations across
30 applications and cut daily sanity checks from 3–4 hours to under 30 minutes.

*For: operations and SRE teams, engineering managers*

### Data integrity is an engineering problem

Why "the dashboard is wrong" is usually a design failure upstream, and how to
fix it with validation built into the workflow and safe repair tooling. Drawn
from correcting tens of thousands of legacy data gaps in a release platform.

*For: backend engineers, data and platform teams*

### Building internal tools people actually use

Designing an internal platform in Django: schema design, SSO and role-based
access, REST APIs for other teams, and shipping eight major versions without
breaking the people who depend on it.

*For: backend developers, students building their first real system*

### What build failures can teach you

Turning a stream of failed builds into categories, patterns and fixes. The
story of a Build Failure Analysis platform that helped bring monthly failures
down from 40–60 to under 20.

*For: DevOps and release engineers, QA teams*

### From production support to platform engineering

A career talk for students and early-career engineers: what support and
operations roles really teach you, and how to grow from fixing incidents to
designing the systems that prevent them.

*For: final-year students, freshers, early-career engineers*
